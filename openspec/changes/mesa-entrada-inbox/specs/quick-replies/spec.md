## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Municipal quick replies
Operators SHALL insert active quick-reply templates into the composer (org-wide or area-scoped). Admin MAY manage templates in this change or via seed + simple settings — at minimum operators can use seeded replies.

#### Scenario: Insert quick reply into composer
- **WHEN** an operator picks a quick reply shortcut on a thread
- **THEN** the composer body is filled with that template text ready to send
